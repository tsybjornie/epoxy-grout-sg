The three questions to ask any grouting contractor

1. Which product exactly — brand and name, not just "epoxy"? Real epoxy is two or three parts mixed on site (ours: Ardex, Sika, Mapei). 2. How deep is the old grout removed, and will you photograph it? At least two-thirds of joint depth; we photograph every joint. 3. How long is the warranty? Most of the trade offers 2 years; CLEANGROUT offers 5, written, any job size.

The process — one day in your home

Rake out old grout to depth (photographed) → clean and dry the joints → apply two-part epoxy mixed on site in your chosen colour → cure overnight, certificate issued with photos and batch numbers. Most homes: in by nine, out by six.
