Why Tampines Homeowners Choose Epoxy Grout

🏢 Perfect for HDB Bathrooms

Tampines has some of Singapore's oldest HDB estates (built 1980s-90s). Cement grout in these flats is 20-30 years old — moldy, cracked, and discoloured. Epoxy grout is the permanent fix.

💧 Singapore's Humidity

Singapore's 80%+ humidity causes cement grout to grow mold within 3-5 years. Epoxy grout is non-porous — mold literally cannot grow on it. Zero maintenance for 15-20 years.

🏗️ Condo & EC Ready

We serve Tampines condos including Treasure at Tampines, Tampines GreenCourt, The Tapestry, and all ECs. Our team is experienced with condo management access requirements.
