Cost per year is the equivalent annual cost at a 5% discount rate — the standard way to compare purchases with different lifespans. Epoxy is the most expensive option on the day and the cheapest one by more than double over its life.

Why it doesn't go black

Cement grout is porous. It drinks water, and mould grows inside it where no amount of scrubbing reaches. Epoxy has effectively zero water absorption — there is nothing for mould to live in, so it stays the colour you chose.

Why waiting gets expensive fast

Open joints let water past the tile into the screed. While it's cosmetic, it's a few hundred dollars. Once it reaches the waterproofing membrane it becomes a hacking job — tiles out, membrane re-laid, tiles back. The jump is not gradual.

Why we won't be your cheapest quote

Across five quotes on one job, the lowest is usually the one that under-scoped it. The only ways out of an underbid are thinner material, shallower removal, or walking off. We'd rather show our arithmetic and lose on price.

What we actually use

Ardex WA, Sika SikaCeram, Mapei Kerapoxy, Laticrete SpectraLOCK — true two-part epoxy, named on your quote before you commit. The tubs are in your house and the receipt is yours to see. Anyone quoting "premium epoxy" without a product name is leaving themselves room.

The 45-minute rule

Once mixed, epoxy is workable for about 45 minutes, and the haze has to come off the tile face inside that window or it sets permanently on your tiles. That's why we work in controlled sections, and why a rushed epoxy job is worse than no epoxy job.

Straight answers

The things people ask before booking.

What will my place actually cost?

Use the calculator above — it runs the same tariff we quote from, so it isn't an estimate that moves later. For reference: an HDB bathroom floor alone is around $550; floor plus shower walls is $1,050 to $1,250; floor plus every wall is a two-to-three day job at $2,200 and up, because 300×300 walls hold roughly 155 metres of joint to rake out. A full 4-room flat of newly laid tiles needing first-time grouting is usually $2,300 to $2,700.

My tiles are new and not grouted yet. Do you do that?

Yes, and it's the cheapest time to do it — around 40% less than re-grouting, because there is no removal stage at all. If your tiler has laid tiles and left the joints open, going epoxy now means you never re-grout that flat. Send us the tile sizes room by room and we'll break the quote down per room.

Why does tile size change the price?

Because you're paying for joints, not floor. A square metre of 600×600 contains 3.33 metres of joint. The same square metre in 300×300 contains 6.67 — double the removal, double the packing, double the tooling. Large format like 1200×600 is the cheapest to grout at 2.5 m per square metre.

Can you match my existing grout colour?

Yes. We bring the colour chart and match against your tile in your lighting, not from a photo. Most people end up one shade darker than the original — that's the colour it will still be in ten years, rather than the colour cement was on day one.

How long, and can we still use the bathroom?

One bathroom is a single day. A full flat is two to three. Joints are walkable after about 8 hours and fully cured in 24. We work around you — one toilet at a time if you only have one.

Is epoxy ever the wrong choice?

Yes, and we'll say so. Movement joints, internal corners and tile-to-sanitaryware junctions need flexible silicone, not rigid epoxy — epoxy there will crack. Cracked or drummy tiles need fixing first; grout will not hold a loose tile down. And if your real problem is a failed waterproofing membrane, re-grouting is not the fix, and we'll tell you that instead of taking the job.

Someone quoted me much less. What am I missing?

Usually one of four things: single-part "epoxy-look" acrylic instead of true two-part epoxy; skimming new grout over old instead of raking out to depth; walls or the shower area quietly excluded; or a site fee and minimum charge that appear later. Ask any quote for the product name, the removal depth, and whether the number is fixed. Those three questions separate the quotes.

Do you cover Johor Bahru?

Yes, on the same published tariff with 10% off. Scheduling is less flexible because we batch JB jobs, so give us a few days of options.
